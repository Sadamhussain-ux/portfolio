Case 01 · Open Weaver · AI product

Open Weaver Studio

Problem
Small businesses need a website, but agencies cost time and money, and DIY builders still need design and technical skills.
Solution
Enter a business name and description; AI generates six themed websites. Pick one, add a domain, pay monthly or yearly and go live. A dedicated developer monitors and supports each site.
My role

Result
1,000+ businesses published their websites with no coding, quickly and at lower cost.

Case 02 · Open Weaver · Design system

Studio Theme System

Problem
The six websites generated for one business can't look alike; each needs its own typography, colours and light or dark look.
Solution
57 themes mapped to business categories, picked automatically: colour pairs and surfaces, a display/heading/content type scale from one base size, and widget styles. Custom themes for brand colours.
My role
Colour mapping, the type scale, the full theme set, the theme picker and the custom theme flow.
Result
Each website gets its own personality, components stay consistent, and owners can still make it their own.

About
