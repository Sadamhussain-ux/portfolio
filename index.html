About me

From pixels to code to systems.

I started my career as a graphic designer, learned HTML and CSS to build what I designed, and grew into UX, where research and systems thinking come together.

At Open Weaver I designed Open Weaver Studio, an AI website builder that 1000+ clients have used to publish their websites. I owned UX research, high-fidelity design, the design system, HTML conversion and prototyping with Figma Make and Claude Code.

Earlier, I led a 28-member UI team at Xerago, delivering campaign front-ends across browsers and devices.
